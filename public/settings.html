Active sessions

Sign-in protection
After 10 failed attempts for the same account (or 30 from one network), sign-in is blocked for 15 minutes. Failed attempts are recorded in your activity log and trigger a security notification.
View activity
Session cookies
Sessions use HttpOnly, SameSite cookies (Secure in production) with CSRF protection. Tokens are never stored in localStorage.
